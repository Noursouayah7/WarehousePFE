BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE DASHBOARD REQUIREMENTS

The platform must include a role-based Business Intelligence dashboard.

ADMINISTRATOR / MANAGER DASHBOARD
Purpose:
- Provide a global overview of warehouse operations.
- Support monitoring, decision-making, and operational performance tracking.

Display the following KPIs:

Inventory & Products
- Total Products
- Total Stock Quantity
- Low Stock Products (products below a defined threshold)

Warehouse Management
- Total Warehouses
- Total Storage Blocks
- Warehouse Capacity Usage (% of occupied capacity)

Customers & Users
- Total Customers

Orders
- Total Orders
- Approved Orders
- Rejected Orders

Shipments
- Active Shipments (requested or in transit)
- Received Shipments

Support & Operations
- Support Tickets / Reclamations
- Inventory Movements between storage blocks

TECHNICIAN DASHBOARD
Purpose:
- Focus on operational execution and warehouse activities.

Display:
- Product movements between storage blocks
- Product quantities
- Warehouse and block stock levels
- Capacity usage
- Assigned operational tasks
- Support tickets created by technicians

KPI DEFINITIONS

- Total Products: Number of products registered in the platform.
- Total Stock Quantity: Sum of all product quantities in inventory.
- Low Stock Products: Products whose quantity is below a predefined threshold.
- Warehouse Capacity Usage: Percentage of occupied storage capacity.
- Total Warehouses: Number of warehouses configured in the system.
- Total Blocks: Number of storage blocks across all warehouses.
- Total Customers: Number of customer accounts registered.
- Total Orders: Total customer orders created.
- Approved Orders: Orders validated by management.
- Rejected Orders: Orders rejected due to stock shortages or constraints.
- Active Shipments: Shipments currently requested or in transit.
- Received Shipments: Shipments successfully received and added to inventory.
- Support Tickets/Reclamations: Number of operational issues or customer complaints submitted.
- Inventory Movements: Number of product transfers performed between storage blocks.

EXPECTED BENEFITS
- Centralized operational visibility.
- Faster identification of inventory risks.
- Monitoring of warehouse utilization.
- Tracking of customer activity and order status.
- Monitoring of shipment progress.
- Detection of low-stock situations.
- Improved follow-up of operational issues and support tickets.
